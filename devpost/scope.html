Catch the AI

A study app where you prove you understood your own notes by catching 3 mistakes the AI hid in them.

The unique kernel

The AI is confidently wrong on purpose, and you can only win if you actually understand. Corrections are judged on meaning, not exact words, and wrong taps count against you, so memorising and guessing don't work.

“Marks show what you scored, not what you understood.”

How it fits together

The core loop, step by step

Paste your notes. The AI teaches a short, clear summary so you learn the right version first.

Click “Test me”. The same content appears sentence by sentence. Tap a sentence you think is wrong and type the correct idea in your own words.

⚠ 3 mistakes are hidden here · 1 of 3 found
✓ When price rises, demand falls. “This one is correct, look again” + why …other sentences…

Correct correction → turns green, counter goes up. Wrong tap → “look again” with a short reason. Hint → shows which paragraph has a mistake.

Your score (including wrong taps), every mistake revealed with the correct fact, and what to revise.

Who it's for

A college student like you, studying at home. They've just read a chapter and want to check they really understood it, often the night before the exam, on a phone or laptop.

How it should feel

Like a game. The best moment is catching the AI being confidently wrong, and you feel proud. It's honest about understanding versus memorising.

What “working” looks like: the 1-minute demo

  1. Paste a short note on the Law of Demand → click Learn → a clear summary appears
  2. Click Test me → banner: “3 mistakes are hidden here”
  3. Tap “when price rises, demand rises” → type “demand falls” → it turns green, 1 of 3 found
  4. Tap a correct sentence by mistake → “This one is correct, look again”
  5. Finish → Review: score, every mistake explained, what to revise

✨ “Oh, that's cool”: catching the AI being confidently wrong.

Why this matters to you

“In exams we memorise without knowing why a concept exists or how to use it. Even toppers forget it when life needs it.”

Also: avoid DIVI MIND's problems with wrong answers and messy formatting.

The boundary

✅ Now: in the proof of concept (4)
  • Pasted notes only. They're the source of truth for the summary, the mistakes, and checking.
  • Learn → Catch (exactly 3 mistakes, banner, tap + type, meaning-based check, counter, “look again” + reason, paragraph hint) → Review (score, wrong taps, mistakes with correct facts, what to revise)
  • Works on laptop and phone-sized screens
  • One session at a time, nothing saved
⏳ Later (4)
  • Typing just a topic instead of notes
  • Accounts and saved history
  • Deploying online (optional for the hackathon)
  • File/PDF upload
✂️ Cut, with reasons (2)
  • Topic-only input (for now): no source of truth, so the AI could invent wrong “facts,” the DIVI MIND problem.
  • Login and saving: not needed to prove the idea in a 1-minute demo.

Notes carried to the PRD

  • Fair mistakes: each one clearly contradicts your notes. No trick wording.
  • Saved correct fact: when a mistake is planted, the correct fact is saved, and checking compares the answer with it.
