How the plan grew

Scope said: “find and correct 3 mistakes, with hints.”
Now we know exactly what that means: tap and type in your own words, a check on meaning against the saved correct fact, “Right spot 👀” vs. “Close! 🤏” vs. “look again,” a nudge after 2 tries, a hint that points to the paragraph, Show answers, a red CAUGHT ✓ stamp, and confetti.

Fairness rules

  • Exactly 3 mistakes, and each one clearly contradicts your notes. No trick wording.
  • The correct fact is saved when each mistake is planted, and checking compares your answer with it.

How we'll know it works

  • “Demand goes down” is accepted as CAUGHT ✓ (meaning, not exact words)
  • “Demand stays the same” shows “Right spot 👀…” and isn't counted as found
  • Tapping a correct sentence shows “look again” + a reason, and the wrong-tap count goes up
  • The nudge appears after 2 wrong fixes
  • The counter is always right, and the 3rd catch → confetti → Review
  • Show answers → unfound mistakes are marked “missed”

How we'll know it works

  • The three numbers match what happened in the round
  • The label follows the rules (e.g., 3 found + 1 hint → Getting there)
  • No score out of 100 anywhere
  • All 3 mistakes are shown with their correct facts, marked caught or missed
  • The revise list includes only missed or wrongly answered mistakes, or “Nothing to revise, case closed 🕵️✅”
  • Next case goes to an empty Start screen

Look and feel

  • 🕵️ Detective theme: magnifying glass, Catch screen as a case file, red CAUGHT ✓ stamps
  • 🎉 Small game moments: the counter going up, confetti on all 3
  • 📄 Warm paper-like background, dark text, one strong accent color
  • 🌙 Readable at night on a phone: clean text area, no crowded screens
  • 🚫 Avoid: messy symbols, long paragraphs, error codes

Other states

Loading, errors, persistence
  • Waiting for the AI: “Investigating your notes… 🔍” assumption
  • AI couldn't respond: friendly “try again” + a retry button, no error codes assumption
  • Persistence: nothing is saved. Refreshing or Next case starts fresh.
⏳ Deferred & non-goals
  • Deferred: topic-only input, accounts/history, deploying online, PDF upload
  • Non-goals: no marks/grades, no trick mistakes, no facts beyond your notes, no login/leaderboards
❓ Open questions (none block the spec)
  • Does a mistake caught after wrong fixes also go on the revise list? assumed yes
  • Can the hint be used more than once? assumed yes, once per unfound mistake
  • Maximum notes length: decide in 4-spec
